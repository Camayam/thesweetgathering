Frequently Asked Questions

The details for a relaxed afternoon of cake and conversation.

Can I cancel my ticket or get a refund?

Ticket cancellations and refunds are not possible. Please make sure you can attend before booking.

What is included?

Coffee, tea, and lemonade are always included in the events. Takeaway boxes will also be provided.

What should I do if I have an allergy or dietary requirement?

Please inform us of any allergies or dietary requirements before booking. Wait for our response before purchasing a ticket so we can confirm whether your needs can be accommodated.

Event information and ticket availability may change. Please check the ticket page for the latest purchase details. If you have a question about access, ingredients, or the venue, contact us before attending.
