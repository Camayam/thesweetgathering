The Sweet Gathering - Cake Swap

One cake to bring, many cakes to try...

Join the Cake Swap
Date Sunday, November 29th
Time 13:00 - 16:30
Place Enschede City Center

Ingredients

A good cake swap starts with a little homemade joy.

  • Bring one homemade, uncut cake (about 10 servings)
  • Coffee, tea, and lemonade are included
  • Takeaway boxes are available for treats to bring home

Instructions

  1. 01
    Book your ticket. Reserve your place at the table.
  2. 02
    Introduce your bake. Share the story behind it.
  3. 03
    Meet and mingle. Enjoy the afternoon, try other cakes, and get inspired with other peoples creations.
  4. 04
    Pick your favourites. Take your favourite slices home for later.
